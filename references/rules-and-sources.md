# Planning rules and source verification

Use the jurisdiction and project stage to locate the applicable rules. Start with land and access rights, planning approvals, building and accessibility codes, local street design and traffic rules, environmental and utility constraints, and who must maintain the result. Record the exact clause, current edition, status, geographic scope, and how it affects the proposal. Record unknown requirements as items to verify during design.

Binding minimum requirements set a floor. Local needs can justify additional amenities and services. Check that they are compatible with the applicable rules. The country references below are adapted from the Russian edition's November 13, 2026 snapshot. Verify the current official text, amendments, and applicability before using any entry.

## Russia

Map parcels, ownership, permitted uses, easements, and special-use zones. Find the municipal general plan, land-use and development regulations, regional and local urban planning standards, public-realm rules, and traffic scheme. In courtyards, establish shared and individual property rights. Check documents relevant to the actual measure, including heritage, vegetation, facades and signs, utilities, and collection of waste.

### Municipal public-realm rules

[Article 58 of Federal Law No. 33-FZ](https://rosstat.gov.ru/storage/mediabank/FZ_33-20032025.pdf) provides for municipal public-realm rules. Find the municipality's own act through its council, administration, or planning information system. Record the decision number, date, current edition, amendments, appendices, and link. Check that the site falls within the act's scope. For the work proposed, look for provisions on trees and excavation, play areas and furnishings, entries and facades, signs, snow, cleaning, waste, dogs, events, runoff, and slope protection. Check temporary access, resident participation, and responsibility for upkeep.

Use the rules to identify permissible activities and the conditions for siting, construction, and upkeep. Where the city has official adjoining-area plans, overlay them to identify maintenance responsibilities. Ask the municipality for missing documents. Rosstandart entries help locate editions and amendments. Check legal status before treating a clause as binding.

### Documents by subject

- **Planning and housing.** The source edition records [SP 42.13330.2026](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/f6917ab4-63d8-4ecb-9794-0b4990ba3b99) taking effect on July 12, 2026 in place of the 2016 edition. [SP 476.1325800.2020](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/4fed603a-e722-4601-b741-7541253109f2) covers new residential neighborhoods and comprehensive redevelopment of existing ones. Check the scope before applying requirements for new construction to a small retrofit.
- **Sidewalks, streets, stops, and crossings.** Check [SP 396.1325800.2018](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/b06a81a0-0143-4299-aac1-764b05cf4242) for new and reconstructed streets and [GOST R 52289-2019](https://protect.gost.ru/gost/details/94160176-3252-4e7d-8534-2d172e6aa0de) for signs, markings, and signals. Confirm the traffic plan and agree proposed changes with the road authority. Use compliant traffic control where required.
- **Accessible entrances, routes, and public buildings.** Check [SP 59.13330.2020](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/9a314146-2e12-458d-a3dc-914eaa364d51/) and current amendments for the relevant building and every applicable part of the route from entry to service.
- **Public-realm works and play.** Check [SP 82.13330.2016](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/8d5a8ef5-f450-4356-ac88-72cd17c416cf) and the current equipment standards it references. Use observation and consultation to identify which kinds of play are needed.
- **Public toilets and care facilities.** [Rospotrebnadzor's guidance](https://zpp.rospotrebnadzor.ru/Forum/Appeals/Details/57626) points to sanitary rule SP 2.1.3678-20 for siting, layout, and operation. For new or remodeled buildings check [SP 118.13330.2022](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/ced01945-2f53-47c9-bdb8-4875c2b9800e) and [SP 59.13330.2020](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/9a314146-2e12-458d-a3dc-914eaa364d51/). For sewer connections check [SP 32.13330.2018](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/cf3b6ea5-c63b-4aa4-9dd3-4295fcaef945). Treat free menstrual products, feeding areas, and easy access to supplies as service improvements unless a specific local requirement applies.
- **Waterfronts.** Check the Water Code, water protection and coastal strips, flood zones, and land rights. For a proposed waterfront check [SP 398.1325800.2018](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/dc6aa095-9983-4133-be3f-7c6f319e4afb). At an official beach check [SanPiN 2.1.3684-21](https://86.rospotrebnadzor.ru/news/zonyi-rekreaczii-na-vodnyix-obektax-dolzhnyi-sootvetstvovat-sanitarnyim-trebovaniyam.html) including toilets. Establish which approvals apply to work in the channel and which apply to an existing upper-bank path.
- **Lighting and drainage.** Check [SP 52.13330.2016](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/9c938e6d-bd3f-4c81-904f-ba8d4c14357b) for outdoor lighting and [SP 32.13330.2018](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/cf3b6ea5-c63b-4aa4-9dd3-4295fcaef945) for applicable drainage networks. Use calculations and utility plans to establish fixture locations and runoff paths.
- **Basements and civil protection.** For possible underground conversions, check [SP 88.13330.2022](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/95171860-edb7-41d1-8c5a-c78dbc9ff50c), [GOST R 42.4.16-2023](https://protect.gost.ru/gost/details/3e396477-59c4-4a36-8553-b38f194eb5ce), and [EMERCOM's survey guidance](https://mchs.gov.ru/deyatelnost/grazhdanskaya-oborona/akty-rekomendatelnogo-haraktera/metodicheskie-rekomendacii/metodicheskie-rekomendacii-po-provedeniyu-inventarizacii-i-obsledovaniya-tehnicheskogo-sostoyaniya-zaglublennyh-pomeshcheniy-i-sooruzheniy-podzemnogo-prostranstva-za-isklyucheniem-obektov-metropolitena-prisposoblyaemyh-dlya-ukrytiya-naseleniya-v-tom-chisle-podvalnyh-pomeshcheniy-chastnyh-domovladeniy-pri-ih-uchete-v-raschetah-ukryvaemyh). Check construction, entrances, utilities, ventilation, water, fire safety, and the required protection type. A responsible authority confirms whether a facility is operational. For existing facilities see [EMERCOM's rules for peacetime use](https://mchs.gov.ru/dokumenty/741) and preserve readiness. Check use and rights separately. A services basement may be common property while a different space may have a separate owner. See the [prosecutor's explanation of Housing Code Article 36](https://epp.genproc.gov.ru/ru/proc_32/activity/legal-education/explain/e306525/) and verify the particular building's records.
- **Competitions and contracts.** For public funds, establish the lawful selection process for design and construction. [Article 24 of Federal Law No. 44-FZ](https://mintrud.gov.ru/docs/laws/116) covers competitive procurement methods. Check the procurement process separately from a contest for concepts. For architectural projects, check [Article 3 of Federal Law No. 169-FZ](https://duma.consultant.ru/documents/1160263?items=1&page=18) and any applicable local competition requirements.

### Sanitary rules

- Check [SanPiN 2.1.3684-21](https://55.rospotrebnadzor.ru/Files/%D0%A1%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%9F%D0%B8%D0%BD%203684.pdf) for site maintenance, waste storage, water, and facilities on official beaches. [Rospotrebnadzor's explanation](https://28.rospotrebnadzor.ru/content/1/17061/info%4028.rospotrebnadzor.ru) covers waste sites. Verify any distances or dimensions in the current text and against conditions on site.
- Check [SP 2.1.3678-20](https://55.rospotrebnadzor.ru/Files/%D0%A1%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%9F%D0%B8%D0%BD%203678.pdf) for public toilets and relevant services, sport, and recreation. Confirm sections and amendments for the facility.
- Check [SanPiN 1.2.3685-21](https://zpp.rospotrebnadzor.ru/news/federal/512693) and local quiet-hours rules where work or new activities may affect homes. Use evidence suitable for estimating noise.
- On school, daycare, beach, food-service, or healthcare sites, find current sanitary requirements for that use and verify whether they cover the intervention. An ordinary neighboring sidewalk can have a different scope.

Check fire access, sanitary constraints, utility protection, maintenance, and permits where applicable. Cite the relevant documents alongside the proposal.

## Methods and voluntary guidance

- [VEB.RF, national master planning standard](https://veb.ru/natsionalnyy-standart-master-planov/) links analysis and design to phasing, funding, and review. It was developed for cities and urban regions. Adapt the approach to the scale of the site.
- [VEB.RF, master planning program](https://veb.ru/biznesu/finansirovaniye-proyektov/master-plany/) describes work on a legal basis as under development in the source edition. Check adopted legal text before describing a procedure as in force.
- [WHO, UNICEF, and UN-Habitat, 2026 child-friendly public spaces guide](https://www.who.int/westernpacific/publications/i/item/9789240116313) covers safety, play, access, health, equity, and sustainability. Use it to consider play across the public realm and verify permission separately for opening school grounds.
- [UN-Habitat, site-specific public space assessment](https://unhabitat.org/public-space-site-specific-assessment-guidelines-to-achieve-quality-public-spaces-at-neighbourhood) brings together observation, resident views, and place-based decisions. Use it to compare conditions before and after work and see who uses a place. Determine walking catchments locally.
- [UK Department for Transport, Inclusive Mobility](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/inclusive-mobility-making-transport-accessible-for-passengers-and-pedestrians) covers accessible pedestrian routes including during street works. Use it to frame questions about temporary access and confirm local dimensions.
- [International Union of Architects, competition guidance](https://www.uia-architectes.org/en/commission/international-architecture-competitions_uia/) covers briefs, criteria, juries, finalist compensation, authors' rights, and what happens to the winning design. Verify local contracts and procurement separately.

## International examples

- [UN-Habitat, International Guidelines on Urban and Territorial Planning](https://unhabitat.org/international-guidelines-on-urban-and-territorial-planning) and the [synopsis](https://unhabitat.org/international-guidelines-on-urban-and-territorial-planning-a-synopsis) give a voluntary framework for connected, accessible, sustainable planning, participation, and implementation.
- [Uzbekistan, Law No. ZRU-1179 of September 28, 2026](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8519124) is an example of a jurisdiction defining a master plan in planning law and requiring proposals and ways to deliver them. Verify rules in the jurisdiction where the project is located.
- [Scotland, Period Products (Free Provision) Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2021/1/contents) and [Edinburgh's access arrangements](https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/cost-living/free-period-products) illustrate a legal duty to make period products available and one approach using libraries and community facilities. Check the specific duty or service model applicable to the new site.
