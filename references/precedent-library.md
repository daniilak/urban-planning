# Precedents for urban design and public services

Most examples are built or operational. The example under construction is marked. Verify the current status through the city, operator, or designer. For each new precedent, record what the primary source establishes, the problem addressed, what may transfer, and what needs local verification.

## Everyday amenities

- [The Tokyo Toilet, Nishisando](https://tokyotoilet.jp/en/nishisando/). An operating public toilet with infant care, an accessible stall, a child-sized toilet, an adult changing table, and sinks at different heights. The project also describes cleaning. Consider combining several accessible amenities and verify connections, room sizes, and maintenance locally.
- [Edinburgh, free period products](https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/cost-living/free-period-products). Libraries, community centers, and sports facilities distribute pads, tampons, and reusable items. Most items can be taken without asking staff. Some reusable items are issued at a desk. Consider discreet distribution and restocking. Scotland has [separate legislation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2021/1/contents).
- [Sydney, public toilets](https://www.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/public-health-safety-programs/public-toilets). The city maps toilets and opening hours, including automated facilities and adult changing places with hoists. Consider a network of services. Check inspection, maintenance, and manual exit arrangements for automated facilities.

## Walking routes, schools, and parks

- [Barcelona, safer spaces around schools](https://bcnroc.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/jspui/bitstream/11703/131117/1/Comunicacio_EN.pdf). Built changes give children and caregivers more room, reduce conflicts with vehicles, and add planting and play. Consider a package with a safe crossing, meeting place, and recognizable route.
- [Kazan, Svetlaya Dolina Park](https://parkikazani.ru/park/park-u-zhk-svetlaya-dolina/). The park repaired a break in a riverside walk and added entrances from several directions. Quiet gardens, play, sports, and dog activities occupy different spaces. Verify land rights and space before adapting any crossing, bank work, or facilities.
- [Kazan, URAM](https://urampark.ru/). Outdoor sports, indoor activities, and cultural spaces operate together. Consider the mix of everyday sports and events in proportion to available area and management capacity.
- [Sapporo, winter street measures](https://www.city.sapporo.jp/kensetsu/yuki/yukishisetsu/05.html). The city describes pavement heating and other snow management methods. Consider targeted heating where clearance and drainage cannot resolve a persistent problem.
- [Vienna, Einsiedlerpark](https://www.wien.gv.at/freizeit/einsiedlerpark). Play areas were adapted for different groups of children, seating and sightlines improved, and part of the park serves school and daycare during the day. Observe who might lose space when uses overlap.
- [Paris, Oasis schoolyards](https://www.paris.fr/pages/les-cours-oasis-7389). The source edition recorded more than 240 yards redesigned with shade, vegetation, stormwater measures, and varied activities by 2026. Some open on Saturdays and may open by special decision during heat alerts. Check permission, staffing, and maintenance for opening school grounds.
- [Vienna, dog exercise areas](https://www.wien.gv.at/freizeit/hundeauslaufzonen). The city maps designated areas with details including type, area, enclosure, and water. Consider separating dog walking from conflicting uses and check local demand, rules, and cleanup.

## Cycling, deliveries, and wayfinding

- [Utrecht, bicycle parking](https://www.utrecht.nl/city-of-utrecht/mobility/cycling/bicycle-parking). The city maps bicycle parking near transit and in neighborhoods and reports availability. Consider the connection between routes, short stays, and secure storage.
- [New York City, loading zones](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/motorist/loading-zones.shtml). Residential curb space is assigned for deliveries, taxis, and passenger drop-offs. Consider allocation by place and time, with local signs and traffic rules.
- [New York City, WalkNYC](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/pedestrians/walknyc.shtml). Maps and signs at junctions, interchanges, and public places show streets, estimated walking times, toilets, and accessible services. Consider where people choose a direction and which information they need there.

## Water, planting, and projects in development

- [Copenhagen, Hans Tavsens Park](https://www.kk.dk/borger/bolig-og-byggeri/anlaegsprojekter/hans-tavsens-park-og-hans-tavsens-gade). Under construction in the source edition. The design combines walking and rest with cloudburst management. Consider how drainage can also provide a public amenity and verify soils, runoff, and utilities locally.
- [New York City, cooling centers](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/em/html/beat-the-heat/beattheheat_map.html). The city opens and maps cool spaces during dangerous heat. Sites have individual access conditions and hours. Consider a network of existing spaces, shade, and water and check opening and access for the day in question.

Search beyond this list for the problem at hand. Check evidence of completion, benefit, and scale. Distinguish built, under construction, designed, and proposed work. Record original ideas separately and assess them against local need, fit, and operation.
